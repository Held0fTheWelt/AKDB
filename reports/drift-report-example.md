# Example: drift report

| Signal | Status | Evidence | Recommended action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ADR-009 versus current module paths | Review required | Decision revision and registered source paths | Confirm whether the boundary changed intentionally |

These are review signals with provenance, not automatic truth claims or source mutations.
